01

Accounting equation & golden rules

Accountancy · 8 min read

Assets = Capital + Liabilities. Transactions keep both sides in balance.
  • Debit receiver; credit giver.
  • Debit what comes in; credit what goes out.
  • Debit expenses/losses; credit incomes/gains.
02

Forms of business organisation

Business studies · 6 min read

Compare ownership, control, liability and continuity.
  • Sole proprietor: one owner.
  • Partnership: shared ownership.
  • Company: separate legal entity.
03

Demand: the first idea

Economics · 5 min read

Demand is what buyers are willing and able to buy at different prices.
  • A lower price often means higher quantity demanded.
  • Price changes move along a demand curve.
  • Income and preferences can affect demand.
